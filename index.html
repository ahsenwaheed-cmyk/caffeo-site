We didn't build a robot to replace the barista. We built it to immortalize the craft.

Every great pull has a best day.

We captured it forever.

Manual coffee changes with the barista, the hour and the mood. We work with industry veterans to record their best-day grind, tamp and pour timing as a digital protocol.

The robot carries out that method for every cup. No lines, no waits, no off days.

We're not replacing the craft. We're giving it eternal life.
